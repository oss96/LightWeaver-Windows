Messages over the video read as messages

While the full player is shown, toasts (for example "This part of the video is still downloading.") appear as the player's announcement, top left under the bar (PlayerView.ShowAnnouncement). Today that is a borderless 55 % black pill with 78 % white text, which reads as an on-screen label rather than a message, and nearly disappears over dark scenes. The user asked for a border so it reads as a message.

Each option is drawn over a dark and a bright frame. Values are the real tokens: LwPlayerGlassBrush #B8121214, LwPlayerGlassBorderBrush #17FFFFFF, LwGroupRadius 8, LwText2Brush #C8FFFFFF, the toast's info glyph (InfoSolid, #F08C9C). "Current" is drawn from the code's values; no live screenshot was taken.

Current

#8C000000 fill, no stroke, radius 12, 12.5 px text at 78 % white. Reads like a subtitle or status label, not a message.
