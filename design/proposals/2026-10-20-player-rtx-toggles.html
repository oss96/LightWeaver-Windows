RTX Video HDR

RTX VSR on (accentSoft fill, white text); RTX HDR off and hovered (hover fill, 78 % white text), with its tooltip.

  • What changes. A ToggleButton pair, "RTX VSR" and "RTX HDR", in the style LwBarToggle: 38 px high like the bar's buttons, min width 44, 8 px side padding, 13 semibold like the Speed button, LwControlRadius. Off: transparent, text at 78 % white. On: LwAccentSoftBrush fill, full-white text. Hover and press use LwHoverBrush / LwPressBrush and snap. Tooltip and accessible name "RTX Video Super Resolution" / "RTX Video HDR"; AutomationIds PlayerVsrToggle / PlayerRtxHdrToggle. A click does not take keyboard focus, so Space still pauses.
  • Behaviour. A toggle writes the Settings default (RtxVsrDefault, RtxHdrDefault) and saves; the save re-applies the RTX state to the playing video through PlayerViewModel.ApplyRtxDefaults → MpvPlayer.SetRtxFilters, the same path a Settings change now takes. Settings and the bar always agree.
  • When they show. As in WPF: only while PlayerViewModel.RtxAvailable (an RTX GPU and a source that is not PQ/HLG), and not in the compact bar (detail preview, mini player), where the WPF mini player drops them too. The detail page's preselection bar gets none: there is no source yet to tell SDR from HDR, and the preview column is already full.
  • Why here. The right group holds the picture and track options; text labels match WPF's names and need no new icon. The over-video rule allows the soft fill: it is small and 26 % garnet, not a large accent fill.
