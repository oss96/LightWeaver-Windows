Behavior shared by all three

Click, double-click, Enter, Space and right-click do not open or act on an upcoming episode. No hand cursor or hover highlight. Assistive technology can read the title, status and date as static content.

Available episodes keep their usual behavior. When media arrives and the item refreshes, the badge disappears and normal interaction returns. Dates localize; long labels must fit or wrap, never hide the status. The Arabic layout mirrors leading-edge placement.
