Format badges, collections and Shuffle

Three WPF features the owner picked for 2.0. The handoff draws the detail info panel's technical chip line (movie-details.png: 1920x812, 2.35:1, HEVC Main 10, SDR), which v2 declared but never filled. It does not draw a collection's page, Shuffle for a show or a season, or a menu for a show's card. Each suggestion below stays inside the system.

A. Format badges in the drawn chip line within the system

  • The line under the actions holds the WPF app's badges, as the info chips draw them (LwBoxBrush, 1 px LwStrokeBrush, LwTokenRadius, 11.5): 4K, 1080p, 720p or SD; the HDR format unless SDR; the default audio track's format.
  • The raw resolution, aspect and codec of the sketch stay out of the chips: the Video line below already names them, and the WPF app showed the badges.
  • The line belongs to the version Play uses and follows a version picked in the bar. An invisible chip holds its place from the start, so the page still lays out once.
  • An episode shows the chip line too (without the three lines, which the preview's bar covers). A show or a season shows none.

C. Shuffle for a show or a season within the system

  • Detail page: an icon button beside the actions, the size and style of Download (LwButton, 32 × 32, Icons.Shuffle at 15), tooltip "Shuffle". Shown on a show and a season from the start, so it never moves the row.
  • A show's or a season's card menu starts with Shuffle, where a title's menu has Play and a playlist's (6a) has Play and Shuffle.
  • Behaviour as in the WPF app: the unwatched episodes (every episode once all are watched) in random order become the queue and play in the full-window player; with nothing playable, the info toast says "Nothing playable in here."
