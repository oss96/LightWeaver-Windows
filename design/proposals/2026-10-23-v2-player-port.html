Player port: type a time and Up Next timing

The owner picked three WPF player features for 2.0: typing a position into the time readout, when the Up Next card appears (globally and per show), and carrying the subtitle choice into the next episode. These source-based mockups retain the handoff's elapsed time as plain text (13b), the Up next group with two rows (SettingsPane.dc.html) and no per-show setting. The subtitle carry-over has no UI. The accepted additions use existing controls. Native screenshots are deferred under the development verification policy.

1 · Player: type a time within the system

  • The elapsed time becomes a subtle button (LwSubtleButton, padding 4,0, as the remaining-time button at the other end of the row): hover LwHoverBrush, tooltip "Go to a time", which opens only as the player's tooltip rule allows.
  • A click puts a 76 × 24 field in its place: dim glass (55 % black, 1 px 20 % white stroke, LwControlRadius), LwBareTextBox at 12 in white, prefilled with the position and selected. Its tooltip gives the format: "ss, mm:ss or hh:mm:ss - Enter seeks, Esc cancels" (the WPF string). The seek bar gives up the few pixels the field needs; nothing else moves.
  • No error state: input that is not a time closes the field and seeks nowhere, as in WPF.

3 · Detail page: Up Next for this show new component variant: per-show timing flyout

  • On a show, a season or an episode, a 32 × 32 LwButton with the stopwatch (Icons.Timer) after Download, as Download is drawn. Its name and tooltip say the show's lead: "Up Next for this show: Global (30 s)" or "Up Next for this show: 60 s" (WPF strings). The WPF series pill with its value is not carried over: the action row holds icon buttons of one width, and the page lays out once.
  • It opens a standard flyout (LwPopupBrush, LwGroupRadius, no open animation) below it: the heading "When the Up Next card appears for this show" (LwFilterLabel), a segmented control (LwSegmentTrack / LwSegment) Global · 15 s · 30 s · 60 s · Custom, a 5 to 300 number box under it while Custom is chosen, and the WPF status line (12, LwText2Brush). A choice saves at once, including choosing Custom before changing its value. Long translated segments wrap within the flyout. Focus starts on the chosen segment; Esc closes and returns focus to the button.
