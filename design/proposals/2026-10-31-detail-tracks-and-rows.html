The tracks list and bounded rows

Two changes from the owner's notes: list every track of a movie or an episode in the info panel and show none for a show or a season, and put More like this and Cast and crew each inside a boundary like the info panel, with round portraits. What the page does stays the same: the bar's Audio and Subtitles menus still choose the tracks, and the rows still open, page, scroll and open their full lists as they do now.

1 · Every track, in the tracks block

within the system  The Video, Audio and Subtitles lines under the format badges list every track of the version Play uses, one per line, instead of only the chosen one. The track Play will use is marked with a check in LwAccentFgBrush and set in LwTextBrush; the others are in LwText2Brush. A title without subtitles reads “None”. An episode now shows the lines too; a show and a season show no tracks block, as before.

The page still lays out once. The tracks arrive after the page opens and their number is unknown until then, so the part of the panel below them (genres, overview and, on an episode, the Episodes heading and grid) shows when the tracks are in, rather than appearing at once and being pushed down. The tracks lookup starts as the page opens, beside the item lookup, and comes from the disk cache for a title opened within the hour. Choosing another version from the bar keeps the previous lines, dimmed, until its own tracks replace them; the overview may move once then, which is the user's own action.

2 · More like this and Cast and crew in their own boundary, round portraits

within the system  Each row sits in a panel drawn like the info panel (LwPanelBrush, 1 px LwStrokeBrush, LwGroupRadius), as the owner's movie and episode sketches draw them. The heading, the arrow, the ‹ › paging and sideways scrolling stay as they are, inside the panel; cards start 16 in and clip at the panel's trailing edge (rows clip at the trailing edge). Both panels share the row's height. In Cast and crew the portrait becomes a circle of the card's width (LwDetailPersonWidth, 120, scaled with the window like today), with the name and role centred under it; the cards stay top-aligned, so their tops line up with the posters beside them and the space below them is the panel's. The placeholders, the empty line, the scale above 1440 and the card's own behaviour do not change.
